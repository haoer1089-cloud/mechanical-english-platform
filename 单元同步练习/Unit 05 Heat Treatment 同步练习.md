# Unit 5 Heat Treatment 同步练习

共 10 题，沿用 Excel 的五个字段。课文：《机械基础概论讲义》Chapter 5 Heat Treatment，按实际正文为 9 段。参考 PPT：`课程ppt/unit 5 Heat treatment.pptx`。PPT 页码为文件中的幻灯片顺序号。

## Q01 热处理的定义

**Questions:** Translate into Chinese: “Heat treatment is thermal cycling involving one or more reheating and cooling operations after forging for the purpose of obtaining desired microstructures and mechanical properties in a forging.”

**Answers:** 热处理是锻造后进行一次或多次再加热和冷却的热循环过程，目的是使锻件获得所需的显微组织和机械性能。

**Locations:** Para. 1；PPT 第 3–4 页。

**Types:** Translations

**Materials:** 据 PPT 定义原句与译文设置翻译题。microstructures 推荐译为“显微组织”。这是课文在锻件语境中的定义，保留 after forging 的限定。

## Q02 哪些锻件需要热处理

**Questions:** Mark Y or N according to paragraph 2: (a) Few forgings are produced without some form of heat treatment. (b) Untreated forgings described here are usually low-carbon steel parts for noncritical applications, or parts intended for further hot working and subsequent heat treatment. (c) Desired properties are one factor in choosing a production cycle.

**Answers:** (a) Y; (b) Y; (c) Y.

**Locations:** Para. 2；PPT 第 6–7 页。

**Types:** Practice(True or False)

**Materials:** 采用 PPT 第 6 页 Y/N 题型，按原文收窄第 (b) 项条件，避免把“未经热处理的锻件”的描述泛化为所有低碳钢零件。

## Q03 热处理周期的选择

**Questions:** What factors determine which production cycle should be used?

**Answers:** The chemical composition of the steel, the size and shape of the product, and the desired properties.

**Locations:** Para. 2 末句；PPT 第 6 页。

**Types:** Reading Comprehension

**Materials:** 据 PPT 正文改编。要求回答材料成分、制品尺寸形状和目标性能，不能只列一种因素。

## Q04 热处理的目的

**Questions:** What is the object of heat treating metals?

**Answers:** To impart desired properties to the metal or eliminate undesirable structural conditions caused during processing or fabrication.

**Locations:** Para. 3 首句；PPT 第 11–12 页。

**Types:** Reading Comprehension

**Materials:** PPT 原题。参考答案忠实概括本段，不把拓展列出的每种性能变化都解释为任何热处理必然产生的结果。

## Q05 材料的加工历史

**Questions:** What should be known before a heat treatment is prescribed? If the material’s previous processing is unknown, what should be done?

**Answers:** The previous history or structural condition of the material should be known. If previous-processing information is unavailable, a microscopic study of the structure is desirable to determine the correct procedure.

**Locations:** Para. 3 后两句；PPT 第 11、13–14 页。

**Types:** Reading Comprehension

**Materials:** 合并 PPT 两道原题。previous history 指材料此前的加工经历，不指个人经历或文献史。

## Q06 五种常见热处理

**Questions:** Give the Chinese names of “spheroidizing”, “normalizing”, “annealing”, “hardening”, and “tempering” in this text.

**Answers:** spheroidizing 球化退火；normalizing 正火；annealing 退火；hardening 淬火（本课文语境）；tempering 回火。

**Locations:** Para. 4；PPT 第 16–17 页。

**Types:** Term

**Materials:** 据 PPT 原有方法列表改编。hardening 在一般语境可表示“硬化”，本课文紧接 heating and quenching 的定义，按 PPT 译为“淬火”。

## Q07 加热 保温 冷却

**Questions:** Complete the common heat-treatment stages: heat the material to a ______ temperature, hold or ______ at that temperature, and ______ at a prescribed rate.

**Answers:** predetermined; soak; cool.

**Locations:** Para. 4；PPT 第 18–19 页。

**Types:** Practice(Fill in the blanks)

**Materials:** 据 PPT 热循环正文改编。soaking 在此为“保温”，不是浸泡。把 cooling rate 与三个阶段联系起来理解。

## Q08 根据定义辨认工艺

**Questions:** Identify each treatment from the text: (a) Prolonged heating slightly below the critical temperature range to produce a globular carbide condition: ______. (b) Heating above the critical range followed by cooling in air: ______. (c) Heating and quenching certain iron-based alloys: ______.

**Answers:** (a) spheroidizing; (b) normalizing; (c) hardening.

**Locations:** Para. 5、6、8；PPT 第 20–22、27 页。

**Types:** Practice(Fill in the blanks)

**Materials:** 据 PPT 各方法定义改编。第 (a) 项考查讲义先给的基本路径，不替代该段随后补述的高碳钢交替加热情形。

## Q09 退火的作用

**Questions:** What purposes of annealing are listed in the text? Give any three.

**Answers:** Examples: removing stresses, inducing softness, changing ductility or toughness, refining the crystalline structure, removing gases, and producing a microstructure. Any three purposes stated in the paragraph are acceptable.

**Locations:** Para. 7；PPT 第 24–25 页。

**Types:** Reading Comprehension

**Materials:** 据 PPT 退火正文改编。remove gases 按英文理解为“去除气体”，不照抄第 24 页中文的“消除气隙”。

## Q10 回火与淬火的关系

**Questions:** Translate into Chinese: “Tempering is the reheating after hardening of iron-based alloys to some temperature below the critical temperature range, followed by any desired rate of cooling. The purpose of tempering is the removal of strains, and the reduction of hardness and brittleness.”

**Answers:** 回火是将淬火后的铁基合金重新加热至临界温度范围以下的某一温度，随后以所要求的速率冷却。回火的目的是消除应变，并降低硬度和脆性。

**Locations:** Para. 9；PPT 第 29 页。

**Types:** Translations

**Materials:** 据 PPT 原句与译文设置翻译题。注意 after hardening 所表示的先后关系。第 28 页将 quenching media 误标为“延缓介质”，建议改为“淬火介质”，与本题的 hardening 区分。
