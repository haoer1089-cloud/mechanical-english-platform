## Q01 锻造的定义

**Questions:** How is forging defined in the first paragraph?

**Answers:** Forging is the plastic working of metal by localized compressive forces exerted by manual or power hammers, presses, or special forging machines.

**Locations:** Para. 1 首句；PPT 第 11 页。

**Types:** Reading Comprehension

**Materials:** 据 PPT 定义正文改编。必须包含 plastic working 与 compressive forces，不把锻造描述为熔融金属浇入模具。

**Answers:** hot; recrystallization.

**Locations:** Para. 1 末句；PPT 第 13–14 页。

**Types:** Practice(Fill in the blanks)

## Q03 现代锻造的发展

**Questions:** Translate into Chinese: “High-powered hammers and mechanical presses have replaced the strong arm, the hammer, and the anvil and modern metallurgical knowledge supplements the art skill of the craftsman in controlling the heating and handling of the metal.”

**Answers:** 大功率锻锤和机械压力机已取代强壮的手臂、手锤和铁砧；在控制金属加热及搬运操作方面，现代冶金知识为工匠的操作技艺提供了补充。

## Q04 三种金属变形方式

**Questions:** Complete the statements: (a) Drawing out increases ______ and decreases ______. (b) Upsetting increases ______ and decreases ______. (c) Squeezing in closed impression dies produces ______ flow.

**Answers:** (a) length; cross section; (b) cross section; length; (c) multidirectional.

**Locations:** Para. 3；PPT 第 19 页。

**Types:** Practice(Fill in the blanks)

**Materials:** 据 PPT 分类正文改编。draw out 拔长；upset 镦粗。通过长度与截面的反向变化理解术语。

## Q05 压力机锻造与锤锻

**Questions:** How does the action of press forging differ from that of a hammer?

**Answers:** Press forging uses a slow squeezing action, while a hammer produces rapid-impact blows. The squeezing action works through the center and the entire section of the part.

**Locations:** Para. 4；PPT 第 22、33 页。

**Types:** Reading Comprehension

**Materials:** 据 PPT 对比正文改编。比较 slow squeezing 与 rapid-impact blows，不扩展为“锤锻在任何情况下都不能使内部变形”。

## Q07 拔模斜度

**Questions:** Translate into Chinese: “There is some difference in the design of dies for different metals; copper-alloy forgings can be made with less draft than steel, consequently more complicated shapes can be produced.”

**Answers:** 不同金属所用的锻模在设计上有所差异。铜合金锻件可采用比钢锻件更小的拔模斜度，因此能够制造形状更复杂的制品。

**Locations:** Para. 5 后半部分；PPT 第 28 页。

**Types:** Translations

**Materials:** 据 PPT 原句设置翻译题。draft 在本句中指“拔模斜度”，不能按日常英语译为“草稿”。比较对象在中文中补足为铜合金锻件与钢锻件。

## Q08 两类锻造的优势

**Questions:** According to the comparison paragraph, what advantages does press forging offer, and what shapes can drop forging produce more economically?

**Answers:** Press forgings generally have smooth surfaces and closer tolerances, and presses can be used for sizing. Drop forging can produce many irregular and complicated shapes more economically.

**Locations:** Para. 6；PPT 第 30 页。

**Types:** Reading Comprehension

**Materials:** 将 PPT 的优势对比填空整理为阅读理解，保留双方比较内容。答案依据讲义第 6 段补全 PPT 留空部分。

## Q09 大钢锭为何使用液压机

**Questions:** Why are large ingots forged with hydraulic presses instead of steam hammers in the text? What additional advantages are mentioned?

**Answers:** The work done by a press goes deeper. A press can also work a cooler ingot and produce closer dimensions.

**Locations:** Para. 7，从 “Large ingots…” 起；PPT 第 32 页。

**Types:** Reading Comprehension

**Materials:** 合并 PPT 同页关于液压机与第二项优势的问题。限定为教材情境，不将原文陈述改写为操作选型规范。

## Q10 终锻温度与温度控制

**Questions:** Why is it important not to have the steel too hot in final forging? How is the temperature controlled when many pieces of the same pattern are made?

**Answers:** Overheated steel will have poor mechanical properties when cooled. For repeated production, furnaces are used in which pyrometers indicate the temperature, and it is often automatically controlled.

**Locations:** Para. 7 末部，从 “In the final forging…” 起；PPT 第 31–33 页对应本段锻造讨论，但未单列温控问题。

**Types:** Reading Comprehension

**Materials:** 据讲义正文补充改编，不是 PPT 原题。关键词：overheated steel、mechanical properties、pyrometers。
