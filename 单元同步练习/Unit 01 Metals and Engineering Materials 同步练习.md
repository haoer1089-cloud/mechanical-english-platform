## Q01 金属的优势

**Questions:** What advantages of metals are mentioned in the first paragraph?

**Answers:** Strength, toughness, and appearance are mentioned. Strength and toughness are identified as the main advantage of metals.

**Locations:** Lesson 1，Para. 1；PPT 第 26 页；Excel Unit 1，B2–E2。

**Types:** Reading Comprehension

**Materials:** PPT 原题与 Excel 原题。依据句：“Appearance is also an important factor.” “The main advantage of metals is their strength and toughness.” 讲解时区分外观这一选择因素与金属的主要性能优势。

## Q02 金属的回收

**Questions:** What other advantage of metals is mentioned in the second paragraph?

**Answers:** Metal objects can often be broken down and the metals recycled.

**Locations:** Lesson 1，Para. 2；PPT 第 27 页；Excel Unit 1，B3–E3。

**Types:** Reading Comprehension

**Materials:** PPT 原题，单复数作语言整理。关注 be broken down 与 be recycled。答案限定为本段对金属回收的说明。

## Q03 铁及其合金的应用

**Questions:** Translate into Chinese: “The most important engineering metal is iron (Fe), which in the form of alloys with carbon (C) and other elements, finds greater use than any other metal.”

**Answers:** 最重要的工程金属是铁（Fe）。铁以与碳（C）及其他元素形成合金的形式，比其他任何金属都得到更广泛的应用。

**Locations:** Lesson 1，Para. 4；PPT 第 28–29 页；Excel Unit 1，B4–E4。

**Types:** Translations

**Materials:** Excel 翻译题与 PPT 原句。关注 which 的指代及 in the form of alloys。讲义同句有 “which is the form” 的疑似笔误，此题采用 PPT 版本。译文不加入“自然界中通常以合金形态存在”的表述。

## Q04 金属分类

**Questions:** Complete the sentences according to Lesson 1: All metals can be classified as either ______ or ______. Metals consisting of iron combined with some other elements are known as ______ metals. Copper, aluminum, lead, zinc and tin are examples of ______ metals.

**Answers:** ferrous; non-ferrous; ferrous; non-ferrous.

**Locations:** Lesson 1，Para. 4；PPT 第 30、43 页；Excel Unit 1，B5–B6、B18–B20。

**Types:** Practice(Fill in the blanks)

**Materials:** 合并 PPT 分类题并根据讲义措辞整理。ferrous metals 译为“黑色金属”，non-ferrous metals 译为“有色金属”。不把 PPT 中关于磁性的绝对化表述加入填空条件。

## Q06 合金与合金化

**Questions:** Give the Chinese meanings of “alloy” and “alloying”. Then explain the purpose of alloying using the text.

**Answers:** alloy：合金；alloying：合金化。Alloying is used to obtain required special properties, such as strength, toughness, resistance to wear, or corrosion resistance.

**Locations:** Lesson 1，Para. 5；PPT 第 34、36 页；Excel Unit 1，B11–F11。

**Types:** Term

**Materials:** 合并 PPT 术语定义与 “What is alloying?”。PPT 第 34 页将合金定义为由两种或多种化学元素组成、至少一种为金属的材料。采用该定义，不沿用第 44 页仅限“两种或多种金属”的简化答案。

## Q07 冶金

**Questions:** What is metallurgy?

**Answers:** The various processes involved in extracting metals from their ores and refining them for use are referred to as metallurgy.

**Locations:** Lesson 1，Para. 6；PPT 第 37–38 页；Excel Unit 1，B12–F12。

**Types:** Reading Comprehension

**Materials:** PPT 与 Excel 原题。同步词组：extract metals from their ores；refine them for use。保留这两个动作作为后续追问的依据。

## Q08 金属材料与非金属材料

**Questions:** Complete the classification using examples from Lesson 2: Metallic materials include ______ and ______. Nonmetallic materials include ______ and ______.

**Answers:** Examples: iron and copper; wood and glass. Other examples listed in the paragraph are also acceptable, such as aluminum and steel, or rubber and plastics.

**Locations:** Lesson 2，Para. 1；PPT 第 56 页。

## Q09 材料选择与总成本

**Questions:** Translate into Chinese: “Where the required properties are available in both, total cost becomes the determining factor.”

**Answers:** 当金属材料和非金属材料都能满足所需性能时，总成本就成为决定性因素。

**Locations:** Lesson 2，Para. 2；PPT 第 56 页。

**Types:** Translations

**Materials:** PPT 明示翻译题。Where 表示条件；both 指上文的两类材料；determining factor 译为“决定性因素”。

## Q10 五种机械性能

**Questions:** Complete the definitions: (a) ______ is the ability to resist applied forces. (b) ______ is the ability to resist penetration and abrasion. (c) ______ is the ability to spring back to the original shape. (d) ______ is the ability to undergo permanent changes of shape without rupturing. (e) ______ is the ability to absorb mechanically applied energy.

**Answers:** (a) Strength（强度）；(b) Hardness（硬度）；(c) Elasticity（弹性）；(d) Ductility（延性）；(e) Toughness（韧性）。

**Locations:** Lesson 2，Para. 4 及其后编号 1–5 的性能说明；PPT 第 57–61 页。

**Types:** Practice(Fill in the blanks)

**Materials:** 据 PPT 性能说明与讲义定义改编。总定义：“Mechanical properties are the characteristic responses of a material to applied forces.” 以讲义完整定义为答案依据，避免把延性仅解释为“可拉伸的长度”。
