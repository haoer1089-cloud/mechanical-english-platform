## Q01 典型紧固件

**Questions:** Give the Chinese meanings of “bolt”, “nut”, “setscrew”, “rivet”, and “key” in paragraph 1.

**Answers:** bolt 螺栓；nut 螺母；setscrew 紧定螺钉；rivet 铆钉；key 键。

**Locations:** Para. 1；PPT 第 1–5 页。

**Types:** Term

**Materials:** 据 PPT 首段术语改编。nut 推荐统一为“螺母”，资料中另有“螺帽”；setscrew 推荐“紧定螺钉”，资料中为“定位螺钉”。key 按机械连接语境译为“键”。

**Questions:** Why does paragraph 3 describe fasteners as an interesting subject in mechanical design?

**Answers:** There have been many inventions and a great variety of fasteners. Their materials, tightening behavior, and performance under different conditions involve important design questions.

**Locations:** Para. 3；PPT 第 16–17 页。

**Types:** Reading Comprehension

**Materials:** 将 PPT “Question: Why?” 补足为可以独立阅读的题干。参考答案取自本段，不使用课件中学生另写的拓展短文替代原课文。

## Q04 复合术语

**Questions:** Explain and translate “titanium fasteners”, “close-tolerance fasteners”, and “squeeze rivets”.

**Answers:** titanium fasteners：钛制紧固件，fasteners made from titanium；close-tolerance fasteners：公差要求严格的紧固件，fasteners with close tolerances；squeeze rivets：压铆铆钉，rivets installed by squeezing.

**Locations:** Para. 4；PPT 第 20 页。

**Types:** Term

**Materials:** 据 PPT 原有复合词释义改编，分别体现制作材料、精度要求和安装方式。不要求背诵飞机紧固件数量及价格。

## Q05 螺钉与螺栓

**Questions:** According to the text, what is the difference between a screw and a bolt, and where is the tightening torque applied?

**Answers:** A screw is intended primarily for assembly into a tapped hole and is tightened by torque on the head. A bolt is intended for use with a nut and is tightened by torque on the nut in the textbook description.

**Locations:** Para. 5；PPT 第 31 页。

**Types:** Reading Comprehension

**Materials:** PPT 原题，增加原段扭矩作用位置的追问。依据作者强调的 intended use，不将第 32 页另一个定义段混入答案。

## Q08 可拆卸螺栓连接

**Questions:** Translate into Chinese: “When a connection is desired which can be disassembled without destructing the parts assembled and which is strong enough to resist both external tensile load and shear load, or combination of these, then this simple bolted joint using hardened washer is a good solution.”

**Answers:** 当所需连接既能在拆卸时不损坏被连接零件，又具有足够强度来承受外部拉伸载荷、剪切载荷或两者的联合作用时，采用硬化垫圈的这种简单螺栓连接是一种良好的方案。

**Locations:** Para. 6 首句；PPT 第 49–50 页。

**Types:** Translations

**Materials:** 据 PPT 原句与译文设置翻译题。注意并列的两个 which 从句。tensile load 与 shear load 统一译为“拉伸载荷”和“剪切载荷”。

## Q09 预紧载荷的作用

**Questions:** What are the effects of preload in the bolted joint described in paragraph 6?

**Answers:** It compresses the connected parts to improve resistance to external tensile load and creates friction between them to resist shear load. The text also states that high preload improves fatigue resistance and the locking effect.

**Locations:** Para. 6；PPT 第 52–53 页。

**Types:** Reading Comprehension

**Materials:** 据 PPT 预紧正文改编。preload 统一译为“预紧载荷”。按原段解释其作用，不把第 3 段的设问转换为不附条件的拧紧操作指令。

## Q10 预紧载荷的实现方法

**Questions:** Name the three methods listed for developing the required preload. For the turn-of-the-nut method, what condition must be reached before additional turning develops useful bolt tension?

**Answers:** Torque wrenching, pneumatic-impact wrenching, and the turn-of-the-nut method. For the last method, the snug-tight condition must first be reached. The required additional fraction of a turn is then determined from that condition.

**Locations:** Para. 7–8；PPT 第 54–61 页。

**Types:** Reading Comprehension

**Materials:** 据 PPT 方法正文改编。torque wrenching 扭矩扳手法；pneumatic-impact wrenching 气动冲击扳手法；turn-of-the-nut method 螺母转角法；snug-tight condition 适度拧紧状态。未将教材中 180° 的特定举例推广为通用扭转要求。
