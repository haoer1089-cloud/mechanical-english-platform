共 10 题，按两篇课文顺序排列，沿用 Excel 的五个字段与题型名称。参考 PPT：`课程ppt/unit 2 powder metallurgy（1）.pptx`。PPT 页码为文件中的幻灯片顺序号。

课文：《机械基础概论讲义》Chapter 2，Lesson 1 Powder Metallurgy (I) 与 Lesson 2 Powder Metallurgy (II)。Lesson 1 采用 Definition、Advantages、Limitations 小节定位；Lesson 2 采用正文段落及小标题定位。

## Q01 粉末冶金的定义

**Questions:** What is powder metallurgy?

**Answers:** Powder metallurgy is a processing technology in which parts are produced by compacting and sintering metallic and/or nonmetallic powders.

**Locations:** Lesson 1，Definition 首段；PPT 第 11 页。

**Types:** Reading Comprehension

**Materials:** PPT 原题。两个关键过程是 compacting 与 sintering。不以第 12–14 页的 3D 打印拓展内容替代课文中的粉末冶金定义。

## Q02 净成形与近净成形

**Questions:** What do “net shape” and “near net shape” mean in the context of the first paragraph?

**Answers:** They describe parts made to the final shape or close to it, so subsequent machining is eliminated or reduced. 中文：净成形、近净成形。

**Locations:** Lesson 1，Definition 首段末句；PPT 第 11 页第二问。

**Types:** Term

**Materials:** PPT 原题。利用原文 “eliminating or reducing the need for subsequent machining operation” 理解两个术语，不引入额外的尺寸标准。

## Q03 基本步骤

**Questions:** Complete the process according to the text: Select and produce suitable powders → weigh and ______ them → press or ______ the blended powders in dies → ______ the green compacts → carry out finishing operations if needed.

**Answers:** mix; compact; sinter.

**Locations:** Lesson 1，Definition 第二段；PPT 第 15 页。

**Types:** Practice(Fill in the blanks)

**Materials:** 根据 PPT “What are the basic steps of powder metallurgy?” 及其答案改编为顺序填空。green compacts 指烧结前的压坯。

## Q04 材料利用率

**Questions:** What is the greatest advantage of powder metallurgy stated in Lesson 1? What happens to most unused material?

**Answers:** It wastes very little material. Almost all unused material remains in powder form and can be recycled for the next product.

**Locations:** Lesson 1，Advantages；PPT 第 23–24 页。

**Types:** Reading Comprehension

**Materials:** 将 PPT 的优势问题限定为讲义强调的材料利用。讲义写 “less than 3% scrap losses”，如需补充数值应保留 less than 的限定。

## Q05 工艺局限

**Questions:** Give three limitations of powder metallurgy mentioned in the text.

**Answers:** Examples: high tooling costs, expensive raw powders, variation in density and mechanical properties, difficulty making relatively long parts, and difficulty storing and handling some powders. Any three are acceptable.

**Locations:** Lesson 1，Limitations 列表；PPT 第 25 页。

**Types:** Reading Comprehension

**Materials:** 据 PPT 局限列表改编。可接受列表内的其他三项，但不能将某一局限概括为“无法制造所有复杂形状”。

## Q06 粉末冶金术语

**Questions:** Give the Chinese meanings of “green compacts”, “scrap”, and “tooling cost” in this unit.

**Answers:** green compacts：生坯／未烧结压坯；scrap：废料；tooling cost：工装费用／模具工装成本。

**Locations:** Lesson 1，Definition 第二段、Advantages、Limitations；PPT 第 29 页。

**Types:** Term

**Materials:** PPT 原有术语表改为可作答的术语题。第 29 页将 tooling cost 译为“加工成本”，本题按其与 dies 相关的上下文推荐“工装费用”，避免与全部加工成本混淆。

## Q07 第二篇课文的定义

**Questions:** Complete the definition summary: Powder metallurgy includes producing ______ and objects shaped from individual, mixed, or ______ metal powders. Objects are pressed or molded and may be heated simultaneously or ______.

**Answers:** metal powders; alloyed; subsequently.

**Locations:** Lesson 2，Para. 1；PPT 第 32–33 页。

**Types:** Practice(Fill in the blanks)

**Materials:** 据 PPT 第 33 页原有定义填空整理。保留 simultaneously / subsequently 的区别，即同时加热或随后加热。

## Q08 难熔金属的制造

**Questions:** Why was powder metallurgy the only procedure for producing certain refractory metals in solid form in the situation described in the text?

**Answers:** Furnaces and techniques for completely melting those materials were not available. The metals therefore had to be pressed into coherent masses and sintered below their melting points.

**Locations:** Lesson 2，Two Unique Advantages 段前半部分；PPT 第 35 页。

**Types:** Reading Comprehension

**Materials:** PPT 原题，保留课文历史情境，不能将“唯一方法”当作适用于所有时期和所有难熔金属的断言。

## Q09 多孔制品

**Questions:** How can porous shapes be obtained by powder metallurgy, and what uses are mentioned in the text?

**Answers:** They can be obtained with lighter pressing pressures or lower sintering temperatures. They are used as chemical catalysts, filtering elements, and bearings.

**Locations:** Lesson 2，Two Unique Advantages 段后半部分；PPT 第 36–37 页。

**Types:** Reading Comprehension

**Materials:** PPT 原题增加课文内的用途追问。porous shapes 多孔制品；filtering elements 过滤元件。

## Q10 加热与均质体

**Questions:** Translate into Chinese: “The application of heat results in crystalline growth and the production of a homogeneous body.”

**Answers:** 加热促使晶体生长，并形成均质体。

**Locations:** Lesson 2，Process Involves a Series of Steps 段末句；PPT 第 39 页。

**Types:** Translations
