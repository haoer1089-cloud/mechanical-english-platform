# Unit 7 Antifriction Bearings 同步练习

共 10 题，沿用 Excel 的五个字段与题型名称。课文：《机械基础概论讲义》Chapter 7 Antifriction Bearings。参考 PPT：`课程ppt/unit 7 antifriction bearings（part 1）.pptx`，正文覆盖讲义 Para. 1–7，本组题目也限定在这一范围。PPT 页码为文件中的幻灯片顺序号。

## Q01 滚动轴承的定义

**Questions:** How is an antifriction bearing defined?

**Answers:** It is a bearing in which the main load is transferred through elements in rolling contact rather than in sliding contact.

**Locations:** Para. 1 首句；PPT 第 4 页。

**Types:** Reading Comprehension

**Materials:** PPT 原题。rolling contact 与 sliding contact 是定义中的关键对比。

## Q02 滚动轴承的两个英文名称

**Questions:** Give the two English terms used for “滚动轴承” in the first paragraph.

**Answers:** antifriction bearing; rolling bearing.

**Locations:** Para. 1；PPT 第 4 页第二题。

**Types:** Term

**Materials:** PPT 原有术语填空题。词形采用课文原词，用于与下一题 antifriction 的含义辨析衔接。

## Q03 为什么并非完全没有摩擦

**Questions:** Why does the text say it may be a mistake to describe a rolling bearing as “antifriction”?

**Answers:** Because some friction still exists, although “antifriction bearing” is a term widely used in industry.

**Locations:** Para. 1 末句；PPT 第 10–11 页。

**Types:** Reading Comprehension

**Materials:** PPT 原题。第 12 页的接触变形解释可作辅助，但本题不要求课外推导接触压力。

## Q04 设计约束

**Questions:** What space and service-life requirements must the bearing elements satisfy in paragraph 2?

**Answers:** They must fit into a space of specified dimensions and have a satisfactory life when operated under the specified conditions.

**Locations:** Para. 2；PPT 第 13–14 页。

**Types:** Reading Comprehension

**Materials:** 据 PPT 第 14 页设计要求填空与讲义改编。PPT 另列了承载要求；此题明确问空间和寿命两项，答案直接对应讲义正文。

## Q05 设计中的综合考虑

**Questions:** Name four factors a bearing specialist must consider. Why does the specialist arrive at a compromise?

**Answers:** Examples include fatigue loading, friction, heat, corrosion resistance, lubrication, machining tolerances, assembly, and cost. Any four listed in the paragraph are acceptable. A compromise is needed because the design has to take all these factors into account.

**Locations:** Para. 2 后半部分；PPT 第 15 页。

**Types:** Reading Comprehension

**Materials:** 据 PPT 正文改编。compromise 在设计语境下表示综合折中，不译为原则性“妥协”。

## Q06 选择轴承还是重新设计

**Questions:** Translate into Chinese: “Thus the problem of the specialist in mechanical design is not how to design a rolling bearing but, rather, how to select one.”

**Answers:** 因此，机械设计人员面临的问题并非如何设计一个滚动轴承，而是如何选择合适的滚动轴承。

**Locations:** Para. 3 末句；PPT 第 16 页。

**Types:** Translations

**Materials:** 据 PPT 原句设置翻译题。上文依据是制造商已提供大量轴承类型、尺寸及推荐载荷和转速，不把此句扩展为轴承专业设计不再需要。

## Q07 载荷种类

**Questions:** Complete the sentence: Bearings are manufactured to take pure ______ loads, pure ______ loads, or a ______ of these two.

**Answers:** radial; thrust; combination.

**Locations:** Para. 5 首句；PPT 第 19–20 页。

**Types:** Practice(Fill in the blanks)

## Q08 球轴承的四个基本部件

**Questions:** Complete the list of four essential parts of a ball bearing: the ______ ring, the ______ ring, the balls or ______ elements, and the ______.

**Answers:** outer; inner; rolling; separator.

**Locations:** Para. 5；PPT 第 20、22 页。

**Types:** Practice(Fill in the blanks)

**Materials:** 据 PPT 部件正文与图名改编。对应中文为外圈、内圈、滚珠或滚动体、保持架。采用可见的第 22 页作图示参考，不依赖隐藏的第 21 页。

## Q09 保持架的作用

**Questions:** Translate into Chinese: “In low-priced bearings the separator is sometimes omitted, but it has the important function of separating the elements so that rubbing contact will not occur.”

**Answers:** 在价格较低的轴承中，保持架有时会被省去；但它具有将滚动体隔开的重要作用，以防止滚动体之间发生摩擦接触。

**Locations:** Para. 5 末句；PPT 第 23–24 页。

**Types:** Translations

**Materials:** 据 PPT 原句与两种译文设置翻译题。separator 在这里是“保持架”；so that 解释其功能。

## Q10 装球缺口的利弊

**Questions:** What is the advantage of a filling notch in the inner and outer rings, and why does it reduce thrust capacity?

**Answers:** The notch allows more balls to be inserted, increasing load capacity. It reduces thrust capacity because the balls can bump against the edge of the notch under load.

**Locations:** Para. 7；PPT 第 27 页。

**Types:** Reading Comprehension

**Materials:** 据 PPT 本段正文改编。filling notch 推荐译为“装球缺口”。区分增加的承载能力与受到影响的轴向承载能力，不简单概括为所有方向的能力都提高。
