**Answers:** dielectric liquid：液体电介质／绝缘工作液；thermal erosion：热蚀除。Small volumes of workpiece material are removed after being melted or vaporized during electrical discharges.

**Questions:** Complete the sentences: (a) The breakdown of the dielectric is initiated by ______. (b) The location of breakdown is generally between ______.

**Answers:** (a) moving the electrode towards the workpiece; (b) the closest points of the electrode and the workpiece.

**Locations:** Principles 中以 “Figure 1.2 gives…” 开头的段落；PPT 第 33、39 页。

**Types:** Practice(Fill in the blanks)

## Q08 放电过程的四个阶段

**Questions:** Complete the sequence: (a) Apply ______ between the electrode and workpiece. (b) Dielectric breakdown creates a ______. (c) The workpiece material is heated, ______ and ______. (d) At the end of the discharge, the plasma collapses and molten material is ______.

**Answers:** voltage; plasma channel; melted; vaporized; removed into the dielectric.

**Locations:** Principles 的击穿、放电与结束三段；PPT 第 32–36 页。

**Types:** Practice(Fill in the blanks)

**Materials:** 据 PPT 第 32 页的四阶段总结改编，与后面三段课文同步使用。plasma channel 等离子通道；molten metal pool 熔池。

## Q09 工作液的功能

**Questions:** What are the functions of the liquid dielectric during the process?

**Answers:** It cools the electrodes, maintains pressure that helps remove molten metal when the plasma collapses, solidifies molten metal into small particles, and flushes the particles away to prepare the gap for the next discharge.

**Locations:** Principles 最后一段，以 “The liquid dielectric plays…” 开头；PPT 第 38 页。

**Types:** Reading Comprehension

**Materials:** PPT 原题。与放电后的间隙清理联系起来，不只回答“冷却”。

## Q10 颗粒残留的影响

**Questions:** According to the last paragraph, why must the removed particles be flushed out of the electrode gap?

**Answers:** If particles remain, the electrical conductivity of the dielectric liquid increases. This makes the process difficult to control and leads to poor machining quality.

**Locations:** Principles 最后一段中 “If particles stay in the gap…” 一句；PPT 第 38 页功能题的延伸，对应讲义后半段。

**Types:** Reading Comprehension

**Materials:** 据讲义正文改编，补足 PPT 功能问题未展开的因果关系。不是 PPT 中逐字存在的题目。
