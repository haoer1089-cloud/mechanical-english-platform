# Unit 3 Casting 同步练习

共 10 题，沿用 Excel 的五个字段与题型名称。课文：《机械基础概论讲义》Chapter 3，Lesson 1 Casting 与 Lesson 2 Die Casting Manufacturing。参考 PPT：`课程ppt/unit 3 casting(1).pptx`。PPT 页码为文件中的幻灯片顺序号。

## Q01 铸造工艺的基本步骤

**Questions:** Fill in the four basic foundry processes: (a) ______; (b) ______; (c) ______; (d) ______.

**Answers:** (a) making molds; (b) preparing and melting the metal; (c) pouring the metal into the molds; (d) cleaning the castings.

**Locations:** Lesson 1，Para. 2；PPT 第 5 页。

**Types:** Practice(Fill in the blanks)

**Materials:** PPT 原题。根据课文顺序填写，不将 Lesson 2 的压铸设备步骤混入这道题。

## Q02 铸件的质量范围

**Questions:** Explain in Chinese: “The product of the foundry is a casting, which may vary from a fraction of a kilogram to several hundred tons.”

**Answers:** 铸造厂生产的制品称为铸件，其质量可从不足一千克到数百吨不等。

**Locations:** Lesson 1，Para. 2；PPT 第 6 页。

**Types:** Translations

**Materials:** 将 PPT “How do you understand the sentence…?” 整理为中文释义题。a fraction of a kilogram 表示不足一千克，vary from…to… 表示范围。

## Q03 常用铸造金属

**Questions:** What metals are most frequently cast? Which metal is particularly suitable for casting according to the text, and why?

**Answers:** Iron, steel, and aluminum are frequently cast. The text identifies iron as particularly suitable because of its low melting point, low price, and ease of control.

**Locations:** Lesson 1，Para. 3；PPT 第 7 页。

**Types:** Reading Comprehension

**Materials:** PPT 原题。答案以本段的 iron 表述为准，不扩大为所有铁基材料都具有同样熔点或铸造性能。

## Q04 复杂形状的铸造

**Questions:** Translate into Chinese: “Since molten materials will readily take the shape of the container into which they are poured, it is nearly as easy to cast fairly complex shapes as to produce simple forms.”

**Answers:** 由于熔融材料很容易形成其所浇入容器的形状，因此，铸造相当复杂的形状几乎与制造简单形状一样容易。

**Locations:** Lesson 1，Para. 4；PPT 第 7 页。

**Types:** Translations

**Materials:** PPT 明示翻译题。关注 into which 的关系从句，以及 as easy…as… 的比较结构。此句说明教材中的成形原理，不要求学生推导工艺成本。

## Q05 铸造设备与材料术语

**Questions:** Give the Chinese meanings of “cast iron”, “pig iron”, “foundry”, “cupola”, and “ladle”.

**Answers:** cast iron 铸铁；pig iron 生铁；foundry 铸造厂／铸造车间；cupola 冲天炉／化铁炉；ladle 浇包／浇注桶。

**Locations:** Lesson 1，Para. 5–6；PPT 第 8–11 页。

**Types:** Term

**Materials:** PPT 原有术语表改为术语练习。课文关系：生铁在冲天炉中重熔，铁液流入浇包，再浇入铸型。cupola 与 ladle 分别是熔炼和转运环节的设备。

## Q06 金属型与防粘附

**Questions:** What two kinds of molds are mentioned in the text? Why is the inside of the metal mold covered with carbon or graphite?

**Answers:** Sand molds and metal molds are mentioned. Carbon or graphite is used on the inside of a metal mold to prevent the metal from sticking to the mold wall.

**Locations:** Lesson 1，Para. 7；PPT 第 14–20 页，尤其第 19 页。

**Types:** Reading Comprehension

**Materials:** 据 PPT 金属型正文与讲义改编。讲义中的 “sick to” 及 PPT 中的 “be stick to” 均存在拼写或语法问题，参考答案采用 sticking to，不改变原意。

## Q07 钢锭与钢锭模

**Questions:** Complete the sentence: Special molds for casting large blocks of steel are called ______; the blocks produced are called ______; the process is called ______.

**Answers:** ingot molds; ingots; ingot casting.

**Locations:** Lesson 1，Para. 7 后半部分；PPT 第 21 页。

**Types:** Practice(Fill in the blanks)

**Materials:** 据 PPT 铸锭正文改编。分别对应“钢锭模、钢锭、铸锭”，区分设备、制品和工艺。

## Q08 冷室压铸的应用

**Questions:** What is the application of the cold-chamber process described in Lesson 2, and why is it useful for those metals?

**Answers:** It is useful for metals such as aluminum, copper, and their alloys, which alloy easily with iron at higher temperatures. Ladling metal into the chamber for each shot reduces the time the melt is exposed to the chamber and plunger.

**Locations:** Lesson 2，以 “For cold chamber process…” 开头的段落；PPT 第 31 页第一问。

**Types:** Reading Comprehension

**Materials:** PPT 原题，答案核对讲义。题中所述适用性限于本段举例，关注 each shot 与 less time exposure。

## Q09 热室压铸的应用

**Questions:** What kinds of metals are used in the hot-chamber process according to Lesson 2?

**Answers:** Metals with low melting points and high fluidity, such as tin, zinc, and lead, which do not readily alloy with steel at their melting temperatures.

**Locations:** Lesson 2，以 “For hot chamber process…” 开头的段落；PPT 第 31 页第二问。

**Types:** Reading Comprehension

**Materials:** PPT 原题。依据段内给出的条件与 tin、zinc、lead 举例。该段结尾另出现 Copper Die Castings 的商业措辞，未作为答案依据，需教师核对。

## Q10 压力压铸与重力铸造

**Questions:** What advantages of pressure die casting over gravity die casting are given in Lesson 2?

**Answers:** Pressure die casting is competitive for high-volume production and can achieve better tolerances or surface finishes. Its close casting tolerances also reduce the need for machining.

**Locations:** Lesson 2，以 “Pressure die casting…” 开头的段落；PPT 第 31 页第三问及第 32 页。

**Types:** Reading Comprehension

**Materials:** PPT 原题。关键词：high volumes、tolerances、surface finish。只比较教材明示的方面。
