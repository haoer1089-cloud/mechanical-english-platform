# Unit 9 Cams 同步练习

共 10 题，沿用 Excel 的五个字段与题型名称。课文为《机械基础概论讲义》Chapter 9 Cams，共 4 段。参考 PPT：`课程ppt/Chapter 9 cams（2025）.pptx`。PPT 页码为文件中的幻灯片顺序号。

## Q01 凸轮及其运动转换

**Questions:** What is a cam? What motion of the follower can be produced by the cam motion?

**Answers:** A cam is a machine element with a curved or grooved surface that mates with a follower and imparts motion to it. Its motion, usually rotation, can be transformed into follower oscillation, translation, or both.

**Locations:** Para. 1 前三句；PPT 第 10、14、40 页。

**Types:** Reading Comprehension

**Materials:** 采用 PPT 第 40 页定义题，并结合第 14 页的课文说明。definition 中的 follower 译为“从动件”。

## Q02 凸轮的适用性

**Questions:** Why is the cam an extremely versatile mechanical element?

**Answers:** Because there are various cam geometries and a large number of cam and follower combinations.

**Locations:** Para. 1；PPT 第 11 页优势问题及第 15 页正文。

**Types:** Reading Comprehension

**Materials:** 将 PPT “What’s the advantage of cam device?” 聚焦为有明确课文依据的问题。关键词：geometries、combinations、versatile。

## Q03 凸轮的主要设计用途

**Questions:** Translate into Chinese: “Although a cam and follower may be designed for motion, path, or function generation, the majority of applications utilize the cam and the follower for function generation.”

**Answers:** 虽然凸轮与从动件可用于运动生成、轨迹生成或函数生成，但大多数应用采用凸轮与从动件来实现函数生成。

**Locations:** Para. 1 末句；PPT 第 16 页。

**Types:** Translations

**Materials:** 据 PPT 原句与译文设置翻译题。关注 although 的让步结构，function generation 采用 PPT 的“函数生成”译法。

## Q04 凸轮的形状分类

**Questions:** Complete the classification according to cam shape: ______ or ______ cams are two-dimensional or planar; ______ cams are three-dimensional or spatial.

**Answers:** disk; plate; cylindrical.

**Locations:** Para. 2 首句；PPT 第 18–19 页。

**Types:** Practice(Fill in the blanks)

**Materials:** 据 PPT 分类正文改编。盘形或板形凸轮对应 disk / plate cam，圆柱凸轮对应 cylindrical cam。题目避开原句 “disk or plate translating” 的标点歧义。

## Q05 从动件分类

**Questions:** Give the three bases for classifying followers in the text, and one pair or group of examples for each.

**Answers:** (1) Motion: translation or oscillation. (2) Position of the straight-line motion relative to the camshaft center: radial or offset. (3) Contact-surface shape: flat-face, roller, point or knife-edge, and other shapes listed in the text.

**Locations:** Para. 2；PPT 第 20–22 页。

**Types:** Reading Comprehension

**Materials:** 据 PPT 分类页改编。radial / in-line 在此对应“对心”，offset 对应“偏置”，不将其混同为载荷方向。

## Q06 基圆定义

**Questions:** Translate into Chinese: “In the case of a disk cam with a radial (in-line) translating roller follower the smallest circle that can be drawn tangent to the cam surface and concentric with the camshaft is the base circle.”

**Answers:** 对于带对心直动滚子从动件的盘形凸轮，与凸轮表面相切且与凸轮轴同心的最小圆称为基圆。

**Locations:** Para. 3 首句；PPT 第 24–25 页。

**Types:** Translations

**Materials:** 据 PPT 原句与译文设置翻译题。两项限定 tangent to 与 concentric with 均需译出。PPT 第 27 页另以理论轮廓说明基圆半径，与本句口径不同，正式授课前请教师统一；本题参考译文忠实于所给英文。

## Q07 轨迹点与理论轮廓线

**Questions:** Give the Chinese meanings of “trace point” and “pitch curve”. Where is the trace point for the roller follower described in the text?

**Answers:** trace point：轨迹点；pitch curve：理论轮廓线。The trace point is at the center of the roller and generates the pitch curve.

**Locations:** Para. 3 第二句；PPT 第 26–27 页。

**Types:** Term

**Materials:** 据 PPT 术语说明改编。通过 roller center 将“轨迹点”与“理论轮廓线”的关系联系起来。

## Q08 压力角

**Questions:** What is the pressure angle in the cam mechanism described in the text?

**Answers:** It is the angle between the direction of the path of the roller center and the normal to the pitch curve through the roller center. It is the complement of the transmission angle.

**Locations:** Para. 3；PPT 第 28–29 页。

**Types:** Reading Comprehension

**Materials:** PPT 原题。抓住运动方向和理论轮廓线法线两个方向，避免只背诵一个孤立的“夹角”。

## Q09 压力角过大的影响

**Questions:** Why can the pressure angle not be too large?

**Answers:** A large pressure angle produces an appreciable lateral force on the follower stem. With friction, this force tends to bind the follower in the guide.

**Locations:** Para. 3 末两句；PPT 第 30–33 页。

**Types:** Reading Comprehension

**Materials:** PPT 原题，语法整理。术语：lateral force / side thrust 侧向力；bind the follower in the guide 使从动件在导路中卡滞。

## Q10 间歇运动的阶段

**Questions:** Complete the sequence in a typical cam application: rise – ______ – ______ – possibly another ______. What follower quantity must also be specified?

**Answers:** dwell; return; dwell. The required follower displacement must also be specified, measured in centimeters or degrees in the text.

**Locations:** Para. 4 开头；PPT 第 37–38 页。

**Types:** Practice(Fill in the blanks)

**Materials:** 据 PPT 典型应用正文改编。rise 升程；dwell 停歇；return 回程；displacement 位移。避免把凸轮转角与从动件位移混为同一量。
