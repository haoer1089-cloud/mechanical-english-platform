## Q01 焊接定义的变化

**Questions:** Why is it difficult to define welding? Why are the earlier definitions no longer sufficient?

**Answers:** Welding methods have become very diverse. Not all methods use fusion, some can join plastics, and several do not require heat. Therefore, the earlier definitions based only on melting metals or joining metals by heat are insufficient.

**Locations:** Para. 1；PPT 第 11–12 页。

**Types:** Reading Comprehension

**Materials:** 合并 PPT “Why is it hard to define welding?” 与旧定义问题。依据是第一段按 fusion、heat、plastics、heatless methods 展开的定义变化。

## Q02 课文采用的定义

**Questions:** Complete the definition adopted in the text: “Welding is the joining of ______ and ______ by methods that do not employ ______.”

**Answers:** metals; plastics; fastening devices.

**Locations:** Para. 1 末句；PPT 第 11–12 页。

**Types:** Practice(Fill in the blanks)

**Materials:** 将 PPT 关于当前定义的问题改编为填空。此处考查“课文采用的定义”，不把教材表述标为最新行业标准。

## Q03 焊接工艺的命名

**Questions:** What is the main idea of the second paragraph? Why is there no uniform method of naming welding processes?

**Answers:** There is no uniform naming method. Processes may be named after the heat source, shielding method, or type of joint. The same joint type may also be produced by different processes.

**Locations:** Para. 2；PPT 第 13 页。

**Types:** Reading Comprehension

**Materials:** PPT 原题合并。可用 spot welding、butt welding 作为本段中的例子。

## Q04 软钎焊

**Questions:** What is soldering?

**Answers:** Soldering is the process of joining two metals by a third metal applied in the molten state.

**Locations:** Para. 4 首句；PPT 第 27 页。

**Types:** Reading Comprehension

**Materials:** PPT 原题。考查第三种金属作为熔融连接材料，不要求背诵拓展页的温度数值。

## Q05 软钎焊接头的强度

**Questions:** Translate into Chinese: “Soldering gives a satisfactory joint for light articles of steel, copper or brass, but the strength of a soldered joint is rather less than a joint which is brazed, riveted or welded.”

**Answers:** 软钎焊可为钢、铜或黄铜制的轻型制件形成令人满意的接头，但软钎焊接头的强度明显低于硬钎焊、铆接或焊接接头的强度。

**Locations:** Para. 4；PPT 第 31 页。

**Types:** Translations

**Materials:** 据 PPT 原句设置翻译题。术语：soldering 软钎焊；brazing 硬钎焊；riveted 铆接的。翻译比较结构时补出第二个“强度”，使中文逻辑清晰。

## Q06 压焊步骤

**Questions:** Fill in the four steps of pressure welding in the order given in the PPT: Step 1 ______; Step 2 ______; Step 3 ______; Step 4 ______.

**Answers:** (1) Clean the surfaces thoroughly first. (2) Heat the ends of the metal to a white heat. (3) Press or hammer the ends together. (4) Smooth off the joint.

**Locations:** Para. 5；PPT 第 40 页。

**Types:** Practice(Fill in the blanks)

**Materials:** PPT 原有步骤填空题。讲义先说明加热与压合，随后强调首先清洁；练习采用 PPT 已整理出的操作顺序。

## Q07 加热至白炽状态

**Questions:** Translate into Chinese: “The ends of metal are heated to a white heat—for iron, the welding temperature should be about 1300°C—in a flame.”

**Answers:** 将金属端部在火焰中加热至白炽状态；对于铁，焊接温度应约为 1300°C。

**Locations:** Para. 5；PPT 第 37–38 页。

**Types:** Translations

**Materials:** PPT 翻译内容。in a flame 表示加热方式；1300°C 指句中所说的焊接温度，不应译为“火焰温度”。数值按教材保留。

## Q08 焊剂

**Questions:** Give the Chinese meaning of “flux”. What happens to the flux and oxide particles during the pressure-welding operation described in the text?

**Answers:** flux：焊剂。At welding heat, the flux melts and dissolves oxide particles and other impurities. It is squeezed out when the metal surfaces are pressed together.

**Locations:** Para. 5；PPT 第 41 页。

**Types:** Term

**Materials:** 据 PPT 术语页与讲义改编。答题依据以本段对焊剂熔化、溶解氧化物及挤出的说明为准。

## Q09 熔焊的热源

**Questions:** According to the last paragraph, how can the heat for fusion welding be generated?

**Answers:** It can be generated by an extremely hot flame from an oxy-acetylene torch or by an electric arc.

**Locations:** Para. 6 开头；PPT 第 45–48 页。

**Types:** Reading Comprehension

**Materials:** 据 PPT 氧乙炔焊内容与讲义改编。限于本段列出的热源，重点理解 heat for fusion 与 electric arc。

## Q10 点焊

**Questions:** According to the text, how are two metal sheets or plates joined by spot welding?

**Answers:** They are placed together with a slight overlap. A current passes between the electrodes, and strong pressure is applied at welding temperature to make the weld.

**Locations:** Para. 6 末部，从 “A different method is usually employed…” 起；PPT 第 49 页的焊接工艺介绍任务可在此收束。

**Types:** Reading Comprehension

**Materials:** 据讲义末段改编，用于补足 PPT 第 49 页综合介绍任务中的课文理解。此题不是 PPT 中逐字存在的题目。关键词：slight overlap、current、pressure。
