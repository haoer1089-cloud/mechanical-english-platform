# Unit 10 Machining 同步练习

共 10 题，沿用 Excel 的五个字段与题型名称，覆盖 Lesson 1 Introduction of Machining 和 Lesson 2 Lathes。参考 PPT：`课程ppt/Unit 10 Machining5-19.pptx`。PPT 页码为文件中的幻灯片顺序号。

讲义把 Machining 误续编号为 Chapter 9，本文按 PPT 的 Chapter 10 编为 Unit 10。Lesson 1 有 3 段，Lesson 2 有 10 段；以下段号均在各 Lesson 内重新计算。

## Q01 机加工的定义

**Questions:** What is machining?

**Answers:** Machining is a shape-producing process in which a power-driven device causes material to be removed in chip form.

**Locations:** Lesson 1，Para. 1；PPT 第 8 页。

**Types:** Reading Comprehension

**Materials:** PPT 原题。关键词：shape-producing、power-driven device、chip form。材料以切屑形式去除是答题重点。

## Q02 工件与刀具的支承

**Questions:** Complete the sentence: Most machining is done with equipment that supports both the ______ and the ______.

**Answers:** workpiece; cutting tool.

**Locations:** Lesson 1，Para. 1；PPT 第 10 页。

**Types:** Practice(Fill in the blanks)

**Materials:** 将 PPT “Another feature for this manufacturing process is…” 聚焦为原句填空。讲义同时保留便携设备的例外，题干中的 most 不应改为 all。

## Q03 机加工的两项应用优势

**Questions:** What are the two applications of machining in manufacturing described in the text?

**Answers:** Low setup cost for small quantities, and close accuracies with good surface finishes.

**Locations:** Lesson 1，Para. 2–3 的段首提示语；PPT 第 19 页。

**Types:** Reading Comprehension

**Materials:** PPT 原题。low setup cost 指小批量生产的准备费用低，不应把整台机床理解为低成本设备。

## Q04 机加工的成形灵活性

**Questions:** Translate into Chinese: “By making use of generally high cost equipment but without special tooling, it is possible, by machining, to start with nearly any form of raw material, so long as the exterior dimensions are great enough, and produce any desired shape from any material.”

**Answers:** 利用通常价格较高的设备，但无需专用工装，只要原材料的外形尺寸足够大，就可以通过机加工，采用几乎任意形态的原材料，将各种材料加工成所需形状。

**Locations:** Lesson 1，Para. 2；PPT 第 14 页，并参照第 19 页的优势问题。

**Types:** Translations

**Materials:** 据 PPT 原句设置翻译题。without special tooling 译为“无需专用工装”，不采用 PPT 中文的“无需后续加工”。“any”系教材概括性表达，此题保留句意。

## Q05 高精度加工的作用

**Questions:** Why are some parts given a general shape by another process and then machined only on selected surfaces?

**Answers:** Other processes can produce their general shape, while machining is used on selected surfaces where high accuracy and good surface finish are needed.

**Locations:** Lesson 1，Para. 3；PPT 第 16、18 页。

**Types:** Reading Comprehension

**Materials:** 从 PPT 第 18 页关于 high accuracies and good finishes 的理解题中选取一个有明确课文支撑的问点。

## Q06 机床的三项功能

**Questions:** What three major functions does a machine tool perform?

**Answers:** It rigidly supports the workpiece or its holder and the cutting tool, provides relative motion between the workpiece and cutting tool, and provides a range of feeds and speeds.

**Locations:** Lesson 2，Para. 1；PPT 第 23 页。

**Types:** Reading Comprehension

**Materials:** 据 PPT 正文改编。holder 在 workpiece or its holder 中指工件夹持装置，不沿用 PPT 将其译为“刀架”的标注。

## Q07 刀具分类

**Questions:** Complete the classification: Machines using ______ tools include engine lathes and boring machines. Machines using ______ tools include drilling and milling machines.

**Answers:** single-point; multipoint.

**Locations:** Lesson 2，Para. 2–3；PPT 第 25、30、36 页。

**Types:** Practice(Fill in the blanks)

**Materials:** 据 PPT 分类正文改编。single-point tools 单刃刀具；multipoint tools 多刃刀具。题目保留车削、镗削与钻削、铣削的教材分组。

## Q08 车床的切削运动

**Questions:** Translate into Chinese: “The lathe removes material by rotating the workpiece against a cutter to produce external or internal cylindrical or conical surfaces.”

**Answers:** 车床通过使工件相对刀具旋转来去除材料，从而加工外圆柱面、内圆柱面或锥面。

**Locations:** Lesson 2，Para. 5；PPT 第 45 页。

**Types:** Translations

**Materials:** 据 PPT 原句设置翻译题。旋转的是 workpiece。PPT 中文误写“旋转的刀具”，参考译文已按英文纠正。

## Q09 主要车床部件

**Questions:** Give the Chinese meanings of “spindle”, “lathe bed”, “carriage”, and “apron”, and state one function of the apron.

**Answers:** spindle 主轴；lathe bed 床身；carriage 拖板；apron 溜板箱。The apron contains driving gears that move the tool and carriage along or across the ways.

**Locations:** Lesson 2，Para. 6–7；PPT 第 47–53 页。

**Types:** Term

**Materials:** 据 PPT 部件说明改编。以部件功能核对译名，不把第 47 页的 base 与后文 lathe bed 混作同一句中的部件。

## Q10 光杠与丝杠

**Questions:** What are the different functions of the feed rod and the lead screw?

**Answers:** The feed rod provides tool motion for accurate workpieces and good surface finishes. The lead screw provides the accurate lead needed for thread cutting.

**Locations:** Lesson 2，Para. 9；PPT 第 60–61 页。

**Types:** Reading Comprehension

**Materials:** 据 PPT 正文改编。feed rod 光杠；lead screw 丝杠；lead 在本句的螺纹加工语境中为“导程”。
